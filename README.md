# ScamShield AI — Advanced Scam & Betting Intelligence Platform

A front-end cyber intelligence and scam investigation prototype featuring specialized **Betting/Gambling Scam Detection**, multivariate **Scam DNA**, **Attack Flow Simulation**, and India-centric **Emergency Triage (1930 / NCCR)**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🎰 Dedicated Betting & Gambling Scam Detector
- **Target Analysis**: Scans online betting URLs, fake casino portals, color prediction platforms, and sideloaded mod APKs.
- **Risk Evaluation**: Distinguishes between 🟢 Low Risk, 🟡 Suspicious, 🟠 High Risk, and 🔴 Critical Risk.
- **Explainable Warning Signs**: Identifies unverified UPI deposit gateways, unencrypted HTTP, suspicious TLDs (`.xyz`, `.top`), APK downloads, raw IP hosts, and telegram bookmaker redirects.
- **Explicit Safety Recommendations**: Highlights critical rules (Do NOT deposit money, Never enter UPI PIN, Do NOT download APKs).

### 2. 🧬 Scam DNA (Signature Threat Breakdown)
Models 6 multivariate psychological & technical risk vectors:
- **Urgency & Pressure (%)**
- **Financial Pressure (%)**
- **Impersonation Risk (%)**
- **Suspicious Payment Vector (%)**
- **Domain / Infrastructure Risk (%)**
- **Unrealistic Gains Hook (%)**

### 3. ⚠️ Attack Flow Simulation ("What Happens If I Continue?")
Sequential flowchart illustrating the exact fraudulent progression from initial ad lure to deposit lock-in and withdrawal extortion.

### 4. 🌐 Universal Multi-Modal Scanner ("Paste Anything")
- **Betting / Gambling URLs**
- **General Phishing & Spoofed URLs**
- **SMS & WhatsApp Text Messages** (Power cut, KBC Lottery, UPI PIN trick)
- **Screenshot & Payment Slip OCR**
- **Part-Time Job & Prepaid Task Scams** (YouTube like, crypto task)

### 5. 🚨 "I've Already Been Scammed" Emergency Hub
Immediate actionable triage protocols aligned with India's **National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930)** and **cybercrime.gov.in**:
- Money transferred via UPI
- OTP / PIN shared
- Rogue APK / AnyDesk installed
- Card / NetBanking compromised

### 6. 🗄️ Community Threat Database & Incident Report Generator
- **Searchable Threat DB**: Filter by category (Betting/Gambling, Phishing, Job Fraud, Loan APK).
- **Report Scam Modal**: Crowdsourced reporting of suspicious betting links.
- **Printable Incident Evidence Docket**: Auto-generated legal evidence sheet formatted for filing cyber complaints.

---

1. Open `index.html` directly in any modern web browser.
