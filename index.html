What is Scam DNA?

Unlike generic scanners that only give a pass/fail, ScamShield models 6 distinct psychological and infrastructure attack vectors to explain how fraudsters manipulate victims.

⏳

1. Urgency & Time Pressure

Fabricated deadlines ("Account blocked in 2 hours", "Power cut tonight") that bypass critical rational thinking.

💸

2. Suspicious UPI / Payment Vector

Demands for upfront "registration deposits", "UPI PIN to receive funds", or unverified personal VPA transfers.

🎭

3. Impersonation & Spoofing

Mimicking reputed banks, government utilities (Bijli Vibhag), telecom providers, or known gaming brands.

🎰

4. Unrealistic Guaranteed Gains

Unbelievable multipliers ("100x guaranteed in 2 minutes", "Earn ₹5,000/day from home") typical of betting & task fraud.

🌐

5. Domain & APK Infrastructure

Freshly registered shady TLDs (.top, .xyz), raw IP hosting, sideloaded unverified Android APKs, and URL shorteners.

🔒

6. Credential & Identity Harvesting

Illegal extraction of OTPs, ATM PINs, Aadhaar/PAN cards, or device accessibility permissions.

Top Active Indian Scam Patterns

Cyber criminals tailor attacks specifically targeting Indian payment workflows, UPI apps, and regional schemes.

🎲

Illegal Color Prediction Games

Users are added to Telegram "VIP teacher" channels. Initial small bets win, but large deposits are permanently locked behind "withdrawal tax fees".

Primary Vector: Telegram / UPI 🔴 CRITICAL RISK
🏧

UPI PIN "Receive Money" Trick

Scammer sends a "Collect Request" on PhonePe/GPay claiming you will receive money for an OLX item or prize. Entering your PIN always deducts money!

Primary Vector: UPI Collect API 🔴 CRITICAL RISK
🎬

Part-Time "YouTube Like / Rating" Job

Pays ₹150 for first 3 likes to build trust, then demands "Merchant Prepaid Orders" of ₹5,000 to ₹50,000 in encrypted group chats.

Primary Vector: WhatsApp / Telegram 🟠 HIGH RISK
⚡

Electricity Bill Power Cut SMS

Fake SMS claiming power will be disconnected at 9:30 PM. Victim calls the number and is forced to install an APK or pay ₹10 on a phishing portal.

Primary Vector: SMS Spoofing 🔴 CRITICAL RISK
📱

Instant Loan Extortion APKs

Disburses ₹2,000 without KYC, then steals all contacts and photos from phone permissions to blackmail victims within 7 days.

Primary Vector: Sideloaded APK 🔴 CRITICAL RISK
🏏

Fake Cricket Betting ID WhatsApp Portals

Operates through temporary WhatsApp numbers issuing "Master Betting IDs". Withdrawals are abruptly denied when users try to cash out.

Primary Vector: WhatsApp Links 🟠 HIGH RISK
